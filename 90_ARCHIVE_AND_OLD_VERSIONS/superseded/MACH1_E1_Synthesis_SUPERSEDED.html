1
The Problem

Indian ride-hailing fleets run on CNG — not by preference, because nothing was built for their duty cycle. Fleet duty is 200 km/day, 312 days a year, return-to-base every night. It is the most demanding duty cycle an EV can have, and the one the Indian market has served worst.

Why LFP, and what we admit

Published LFP studies disagree 3.6× on time-to-80%. So per-cell State-of-Health telemetry is specified, not optional — it is the mechanism that makes an 8-year capacity-retention guarantee credible.

6
The Risk We Cannot Hedge

No central scheme pays for an electric passenger car. PM E-DRIVE covers 2W, 3W, buses, trucks, ambulances and charging — not 4W cars. This case is built without a subsidy that does not exist.
