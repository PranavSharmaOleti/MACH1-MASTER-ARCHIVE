### Conclusion Band (full width, bottom ~15%)

**AIRPORT TRANSFERS + CONTRACTED CORPORATE COMMUTE**
Two complementary candidate beachheads for FleetLine E1.

Airport: pre-booked demand, verified operator presence, but lowest revenue per paid km and unverified cost advantage.
Commute: recurring routes, depot charging, but no published trip data and unverified contract terms.
Validation required: fleet procurement interviews, airport cost quantification, depot charging feasibility.

### Typography and Density Notes

- Font: 8-10pt for table content, 12-14pt for headers, 10-11pt for conclusion band
- Colour: Graphite text on warm ivory background; Maruti red for conclusion band and key callouts; restrained blue for data emphasis
- Tables: No cell borders (whitespace separation); alternating row shading at 5% opacity
- Charts: Minimal (single bar chart for revenue/km comparison; horizontal bars for fleet scale)
- Source footnotes: 8pt, grey, bottom of each section
- The slide should be readable in ~60 seconds (not 20) given the density
- All numbers must have at least a footnote reference to source

### Information Density Target

- ~150-200 words of text (excluding tables)
- 2 tables (5-archetype comparison, operator intelligence)
- 1 small chart (revenue per paid km)
- 1 comparison matrix (airport vs. commute)
- 3 metric strips
- 3 source footnote blocks
- 1 conclusion band

This is approximately 3-4x the information density of a typical executive summary slide, which is the intent.
