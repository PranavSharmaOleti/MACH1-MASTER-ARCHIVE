# Change Log: v5 → v6

## What was corrected

### 1. Delhi airport pickup-fee impact (Issue 1)
- **Withdrawn**: "₹290 × 6 trips/day = ₹1,740/day exceeds the ₹800 revenue gap." This conflated an unverified trip count with an unverified fee incidence.
- **Corrected**: Fee is real (₹290/trip, ET/TOI 2025) but incidence (passenger pass-through vs. fleet cost) and aggregate daily impact are unverified. Stated as a cost factor requiring site-specific quantification.

### 2. Fleet data reconciliation (Issue 2)
- Added deployed vs. company-reported vs. target/ordered distinction for all 10 operators.
- Evera: airport presence verified; fleet count company-reported.
- Everest: 23,500+ current (company); ESG breakdown 2,000+ EV / 18,000+ CNG (FY24-25).
- Lithium: 3,000+ EVs, 600+ chargers (company-reported; no independent audit).
- WTi: 2,000+ is a target, not achieved count.
- OHM: 100 EVs deployed (2022, verified); 1,300 ordered (delivery unverified).
- Bharat Taxi: 600 national (company) vs. "up to 40" at NMIA (airport announcement).

### 3. Procurement conclusion (Issue 3)
- **Withdrawn**: "Procurement is consistently asset-light (leasing/financing), not outright purchase."
- **Corrected**: Mixed models documented (owned, financed, leased, driver-owned, cooperative). No single model dominates. Fleet counts are stock measures, not flow measures.

### 4. Evidence-grade system (Issue 4)
- **Replaced** single A–D grade with two dimensions: source quality (S-A to S-D) and calculation quality (C-A to C-D).
- **Reclassified**: Unsourced claims are now S-D/C-D (not Grade D "contradicted"). Only claims with specific counter-evidence are "contradicted."
- Resolved contradictory grades (e.g., "40% Dzire to fleet" no longer gets A, B, and C simultaneously).

### 5. Airport charging (Issue 5)
- **Replaced** blanket "no airport offers general depot charging" with three-category distinction: public charging (exists on premises, access terms unpublished), captive/depot (operator-built), third-party fleet access (no documented evidence, but absence of evidence ≠ evidence of absence).

### 6. Ride estimates (Issue 6)
- Preserved distinction between 591.3M (platform-specific, 3 platforms, cab-only, annualised daily average) and 810M (market-wide, all platforms, cab share of total, annual).
- Qualified AAI passenger throughput: secondary compilation sums to 195.94M; exact 196.24M unverified; passenger movements ≠ unique passengers ≠ road-transport demand.
- Qualified airport passenger-demand conversion: three missing conversions (eligible share, mode share, occupancy) documented. Illustrative occupancy conversion (÷1.4 → 56M–84M) labeled as illustrative, not replacement.

### 7. Source links (Issue 7)
- Added direct, dated URLs for every important external market claim in Part 4 source register (40+ entries with links).

### 8. Market report focus (Issue 8)
- TCO section (2.4) reduced to cross-reference only. Full TCO analysis in separate documents.
- GST, PM E-DRIVE, Maharashtra road tax corrections noted as TCO input changes, not reproduced.
