### 2.3 Airport economics (updated with verified data)

**Verified cost data:**
- Delhi airport pick-up fee: ₹290/trip (increased ~40%). Toll + pick-up ≈ 50% of average short-distance fare.
- This is a real, quantified cost that was previously unmeasured. It directly reduces airport trip profitability.
- No airport offers general depot charging to third-party fleets. Operators build captive hubs.
- BluSmart built captive super-hubs (500+ charging points network-wide in Delhi-NCR). Evera uses captive + public charging.

**Updated hurdle calculation:**
- Airport gross revenue/day: ₹3,600 (from deck, Grade D)
- Commute gross revenue/day: ₹4,400 (from deck, Grade D)
- Revenue gap: ₹800/day
- Airport pick-up fee: ₹290/trip × 6 trips/day = ₹1,740/day (if all 6 trips are airport pick-ups)
- **The pick-up fee alone (₹1,740/day) exceeds the entire revenue gap (₹800/day).** This means airport operations face a structural cost disadvantage that must be offset by other factors (zero deadhead, predictable demand, depot charging, lower empty running).
- This is a significant finding: the airport "margin" thesis must overcome not just the lower revenue/km but also a substantial per-trip access fee.

**Note**: The ₹290 fee is Delhi-specific. Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, and NMIA fee schedules are not published.

### 2.4 TCO (Grade A — unchanged, not redone)

All TCO numbers are Grade A. Key correction from external research:
- **GST**: For Dzire Tour S (small car), EV 5% vs ICE 18% (not 28%+cess). The deck's "18% vs 5%" is correct for the Tour S. For larger vehicles, the advantage is 35 points (40% vs 5%).
- **PM E-DRIVE**: No central subsidy for e-4Ws. TCO must not include any subsidy line for passenger EVs.
- **Maharashtra road tax**: 14% for fleet-registered CNG ≤₹10L (verified). EVs exempt. This gap is real and significant.

## PART 6: FINAL CONCLUSION (updated)

FleetLine E1 remains a credible candidate for airport-transfer and contracted-commute operations. The external research has substantially strengthened the case that fleet operators of sufficient scale exist (Everest 23,500+, Lithium 3,000+, Refex 1,750+, Evera ~2,700) and that airport deployments are real (Evera at Delhi T3, Bharat Taxi at NMIA, OHM at Hyderabad).

However, three critical gaps remain:
1. **Procurement behaviour is unverified.** Published fleet counts do not establish who buys, in what batch sizes, on what financing terms, or at what replacement cycle.
2. **Airport cost advantage is unquantified.** The Delhi pick-up fee (₹290/trip × 6 trips/day = ₹1,740/day) alone exceeds the revenue gap (₹800/day). The airport margin thesis must overcome this structural cost.
3. **No airport offers general depot charging to third-party fleets.** Operators build captive hubs. Access rights, tariffs, and bay counts are not published.

The market model should be treated as a prioritisation hypothesis. The underlying strategic direction still makes sense. What needs fixing is the proof that this is the right initial customer and operating environment.
