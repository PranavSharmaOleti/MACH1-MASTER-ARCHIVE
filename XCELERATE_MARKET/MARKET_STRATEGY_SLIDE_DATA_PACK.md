**Who would buy or lease FleetLine E1:** Fleet operators (Everest, Lithium, Refex, Evera) are the plausible buyers. Individual drivers are unlikely buyers for a fleet EV. Aggregators (Uber, Ola) may co-invest or specify vehicles but do not typically purchase. Airport operators (DIAL, GHIAL) are stakeholders, not vehicle buyers.

**Who would operate it:** The fleet operator or its driver-partners.

**Who would pay for the transport service:** Employers (corporate commute), passengers (airport), or aggregators (platform-mediated).

## PART F: NARRATIVE AND SLIDE RECOMMENDATION

### F.1 Recommended slide structure

**Headline:** Airport transfers and contracted corporate commute are two promising entry opportunities for FleetLine E1 — different routes to managed, repeatable, EV-compatible fleet operations.

**Section 01 — Market signal (2–3 callouts)**
- Airport: ~196M passenger movements (Apr–Sep 2026); verified operator activity (Evera at T3, Bharat Taxi at NMIA, OHM at RGIA); lowest revenue per paid trip-km (₹17.1) but potential cost advantages (pre-booked, depot charging, no surge chasing)
- Corporate commute: Lithium Urban 3,000+ EVs, 25,000 trips/day, 600+ chargers; Refex shifted from airport to B2B; Everest 23,500+ mixed fleet
- Benchmark: 591.3M annualised platform cab rides (3 platforms) — largest volume but least predictable

**Section 02 — Target customer (2–3 callouts)**
- Buyer: Fleet operators (Everest 23,500+, Lithium 3,000+, Refex 1,750+, Evera ~2,700)
- Decision-maker: Fleet-operator founders/CEOs (inferred from press, not procurement docs)
- Ownership: Mixed (owned, leased, financed, cooperative, driver-partner) — no single model dominates
- Procurement: Documented channels (leasing, bank finance, OEM bulk orders, co-investment, repossessed assets) but batch sizes, replacement cycles, buying authority unverified

**Section 03 — Entry strategy (2–3 callouts)**
- Priority 1: Airport (Delhi IGI, NMIA, Hyderabad RGIA) — verified operator presence but pick-up fees, no documented third-party depot charging
- Priority 2: Corporate commute (Bengaluru, Delhi, Mumbai) — verified operator scale but contract terms unverified
- Validation condition: Fleet procurement interviews to establish actual batch sizes, financing, replacement cycles

**Bottom line:** FleetLine E1 is a credible candidate for airport and corporate commute. Fleet operators of sufficient scale exist. Airport deployments are real but small. The airport margin thesis is unproven. Corporate commute offers more predictable utilisation but lacks published trip data. This is a candidate direction requiring validation.
