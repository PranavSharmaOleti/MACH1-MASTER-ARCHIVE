# MARKET STRATEGY — SINGLE SLIDE CONTENT (DENSE)

## Headline
**Airport transfers and contracted corporate commute are two complementary candidate beachheads for FleetLine E1 — different routes to managed, repeatable, EV-compatible fleet operations.**

## Supporting thesis
India's ride-service market is large (591.3M annualised platform cab rides, 3 platforms) but segment-level data is entirely unsourced. Airport and corporate commute offer structurally different demand profiles — pre-booked/recurring vs. contracted/scheduled — that may suit a single vehicle platform. This is a candidate direction requiring validation, not a proven conclusion.

All segment metrics: original deck p3 (S-D/C-D, unsourced). DRIVER ₹/MO = gross fare revenue (trips × fare × 26 days), not take-home income. Revenue/paid km = gross fare per day ÷ paid km per day (not total vehicle-km).

**Market scale:** 591.3M annualised platform cab rides (Uber 840k + Ola 460k + Rapido 320k daily × 365). Alternative estimate: ~810M cab trips (Redseer 27% of ~3B total, CY2025). These measure different scopes and are not directly comparable.

**Airport is not a niche:** ~196M passenger movements Apr–Sep 2026 (AAI; exact 196.24M unverified; secondary compilation sums to 195.94M). If 20–30% of passengers use app-cabs (assumption, not measured), airport trips could be 78.5M–117.8M passenger journeys (not vehicle trips; occupancy conversion missing).

**Airport has lowest revenue per paid trip-km (₹17.1)** of five archetypes. Margin thesis must rest on cost (zero deadhead, depot charging, no surge chasing) — unquantified. Delhi pick-up fee: ₹290/trip (verified; incidence unverified).

**Verified anchors:** Metro drivers ~8 trips/day (Redseer). Avg fares ₹300–400 top-7 cities (Redseer). Corporate EVs ~300 km/day (Lithium Urban, Mint). Uber Black 34M km in 2025; premium AOV +9% vs +3% economy; riders pay 25–40% premium (Uber India 2025; Deloitte/FE). EV share of new 4W registrations: 7.3–8.7% (Oct 2026); Delhi 12.95%. CNG crossed 22.7% of new PV registrations (Apr 2026).

**Growth (mode-level, not use-case):** Redseer CY25 vs CY24: 2W +55%, autos +50%, cabs +36%. Airport passenger growth: -2.9% YoY (Apr–Sep 2026); Delhi +6.2%, Hyderabad -12.8%, Mumbai -9.5%.

**EV suitability:** Airport: moderate-high (predictable duty, captive depot charging possible). Commute: high (fixed routes, depot charging windows). Intra-city: moderate. Inter-city: low-moderate (highway charging sparse). Premium: moderate.

**Charging access:** Airport: public charging on premises (DIAL 12 stations/22 points; Mumbai Tata Power 16 guns/600 kW). Captive hubs (BluSmart 500+ points, Evera captive+public). No documented general third-party fleet access. Commute: depot-based (Lithium 600+ chargers). Highway: Tata Power 6,700+ points across 690+ cities; 10 mega hubs on expressways.

**Demand pattern:** Airport: pre-booked/queue-based (Uber Reserve ~40% of trips end at airports; ~1 in 4 airport drop-offs via Reserve; Reserve grew ~200% YoY). Commute: recurring/scheduled (Lithium 25,000 trips/day B2B; Refex shifted airport→B2B). Intra-city: on-demand. Premium: app-booked (Uber Black 34M km). Inter-city: pre-booked (Uber Intercity top routes published).

**Empty running:** Unmeasured for all archetypes. Deck claims "zero deadhead" for airport but no data.

**Platform economics:** Ola ₹67/day (driver pass, not fleet tariff). Rapido ₹9–29/day (auto), ₹40/day or ₹500/month (cab). Uber commission-based (20–30%) with pilot subscriptions (Driver Pass ₹341/72h). GST: EVs 5%, small ICE 18%, mid/large 40%. Maharashtra road tax: 14% fleet CNG (EVs exempt). PM E-DRIVE: no e-4W subsidy.

## 03 — BEACHHEAD STRATEGY

**Priority 1: Airport (Delhi IGI, NMIA, Hyderabad RGIA)**

| Factor | Evidence | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Verified operator presence | Evera at T3 (55% airport bookings, avg ₹600/trip); Bharat Taxi at NMIA (600 phase 1); OHM at RGIA (100 EVs deployed 2022) | Verified |
| Pre-booked demand | Uber Reserve ~40% of trips end at airports; Reserve grew ~200% YoY | Verified |
| Revenue per paid trip-km | ₹17.1 (lowest of 5 archetypes) | Unsourced (deck) |
| Gross revenue per vehicle-day | ₹3,600 (lower than commute ₹4,400) | Unsourced (deck) |
| Pick-up fee | ₹290/trip at Delhi (increased ~40%); toll+fee ≈ 50% of short fare | Verified (incidence unverified) |
| Depot charging | No documented general third-party access; captive hubs only | Unverified |
| Empty running | Unmeasured | Unknown |
| Demand predictability | Pre-booked/queue-based; growing Reserve | Verified (qualitative) |

**Priority 2: Corporate commute (Bengaluru, Delhi, Mumbai)**

| Factor | Evidence | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Verified operator scale | Lithium 3,000+ EVs, 25,000 trips/day, 600+ chargers; Refex 1,750+ (shifted airport→B2B); Everest 23,500+ | Verified (company-reported) |
| Recurring routes | Lithium B2B corporate; 100+ enterprise clients | Verified (qualitative) |
| Vehicle utilisation | ~300 km/day, 10–12h shifts (Lithium) | Verified (company-reported) |
| Depot charging | Lithium 600+ captive chargers; depot-based charging windows | Verified |
| Contract terms | Duration, renewal, payment structure | Unpublished |
| Published trip data | No corporate commute trip data published | Unknown |

**Why complementary:** Airport offers pre-booked demand with verified operator presence but lowest revenue per paid km and unverified cost advantage. Corporate commute offers recurring routes, depot charging, and verified operator scale but lacks published trip data. Both use the same vehicle platform (sub-4m compact sedan, 45 kWh BEV, depot charging). The two channels diversify demand risk.

**Validation condition:** Fleet procurement interviews to establish actual batch sizes, financing terms, replacement cycles, and whether any operator would trial 50–100 units.

---

## Bottom-line conclusion

FleetLine E1 is a credible candidate for airport-transfer and contracted-commute operations. Fleet operators of sufficient scale exist (Everest 23,500+, Lithium 3,000+, Refex 1,750+, Evera ~2,700). Airport deployments are real but small (OHM 100 EVs at RGIA is the only independently documented airport EV deployment). The airport margin thesis is unproven (lowest revenue per paid trip-km, pick-up fees, no documented depot charging for third parties). Corporate commute offers more predictable utilisation and depot charging but lacks published trip data and contract terms. This is a candidate direction requiring validation through fleet procurement interviews, airport cost quantification, and depot charging feasibility assessment.
