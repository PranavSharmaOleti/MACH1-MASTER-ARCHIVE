### 6. Policy and regulation — verified
- **PM E-DRIVE**: ₹11,900cr, extended to Mar 2028. No e-4W subsidy. e-2W ended 2026, e-3W closed Dec 2025. Charging infra ₹2,000cr continues.
- **GST**: EVs 5%. Small ICE cars (≤1,200cc, ≤4,000mm) 18%. Mid-size/large 40%. Dzire Tour S qualifies as small car (1,197cc, 3,995mm) → 18% vs 5% (13 points). Deck's "18%" is correct for Tour S.
- **Maharashtra road tax**: 14% for fleet-registered CNG ≤₹10L. Individual owners pay 7–10%. EVs exempt. Gap is real.
- **State EV policies**: 8 states verified (Maharashtra, Delhi, Tamil Nadu, Telangana, Karnataka, UP, Andhra Pradesh, Gujarat). West Bengal temporary exemption expired.
- **Maharashtra EV incentive**: 15% ex-factory (cap ₹2L) for first 25,000 M1 transport EVs. 50% fleet EV target for operators.

### 8. Airport economics — significant new finding
- Delhi pick-up fee ₹290/trip × 6 trips/day = ₹1,740/day. This exceeds the revenue gap (₹800/day) between airport and commute.
- Airport margin thesis must overcome not just lower revenue/km but substantial per-trip access fees.
- No airport offers general depot charging to third parties.

### 9. Platform fee correction
- "Uber+Rapido ₹29/day" is incorrect. Ola ₹67/day (driver pass). Rapido ₹9–29 (auto), ₹40 (cab). Uber commission-based with pilots.
- Platform fees, lease payments, airport fees, commissions, and fleet-management charges are separate cost lines. A single "platform fee per vehicle per day" is not a safe universal assumption.
