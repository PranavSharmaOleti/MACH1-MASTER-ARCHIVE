# MARKET STRATEGY — DATA GAPS

## P0 — Would change the slide recommendation
1. **Buyer procurement behaviour**: No evidence establishes who buys (asset owner vs. operator vs. aggregator), in what batch sizes, on what financing terms, or at what replacement cycle. Fleet counts are stock measures, not flow measures.
2. **Airport cost advantage**: Delhi pick-up fee ₹290/trip is verified but incidence (passenger vs. fleet) and aggregate daily impact are unverified. Other airport costs (toll, parking, queue time) unmeasured. Revenue gap (₹800/day) is from unsourced deck data.
3. **Depot charging feasibility**: No airport offers documented general depot charging to third-party fleets. Public charging exists but access terms (tariffs, bay allocation, power capacity) are unpublished. Site-specific investigation needed.

## P1 — Would strengthen or weaken the argument
4. **Verified fleet sizes**: All operator fleet counts are company-reported, not independently audited. Everest 23,500+, Lithium 3,000+, Refex 1,750+, Evera ~2,700 are not verified.
5. **Fleet pricing**: Dzire Tour S official retail ₹6.24–7.10L; fleet pricing unverified. FleetLine E1 pricing not established.
6. **Measured duty cycle**: 200 km/day, 312 days/yr is a design assumption (Grade S-D/C-D). Need 30–50 driver-days of GPS/trip data.
7. **Trip-purpose classification**: No public data on what share of Indian ride-hailing trips are airport, commute, intra-city, etc. The 61%/~58% intra-city figures are unsourced.
8. **Airport app-cab mode share**: No Indian airport publishes app-cab share of ground transport. Deck's 20–30% is an assumption. Proxies exist (Rapido 12–15% of cab rides are airport; Evera 55% of bookings are airport; Uber Reserve ~40% of trips end at airports) but no measured national figure.
9. **App-cab occupancy at airports**: No published data on passengers per booked vehicle. Needed to convert passenger journeys to vehicle trips.
10. **Airport fee schedules beyond Delhi**: Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, NMIA commercial-vehicle fee schedules not published.
11. **Residual value data**: No transaction prices published for repossessed BluSmart EVs. The 10%/26% claim is a management statement (Mint interview, Sep 2024), not audited.
12. **Service-mode split**: Uber/Ola/Rapido car vs. two-wheeler vs. auto split not published. 591.3M and 810M estimates measure different scopes.

## P2 — Nice to have
13. **Segment table metrics**: All 35 deck segment-table cells are S-D/C-D (no source). Trip lengths, km/day, trips/day, fares, growth, EV share, price sensitivity — all unsourced.
14. **Corporate commute revenue/km**: Not separately measured. Premium's ₹28.89/km is the highest but corporate commute may differ.
15. **LFP degradation calibration**: Naumann 13.3 yr vs Lewerenz 3.7 yr spread (3.6×) for LFP in Indian conditions.
16. **Inter-city arithmetic**: 420 vs 340 km/day unresolved (deadhead assumption).
17. **Platform fee verification**: City-specific, dated. Ola ₹67 is a driver pass, not fleet tariff. Rapido varies by mode and city. Uber commission-based with pilots.
18. **BluSmart 4,704 stranded EVs**: No per-vehicle resale/auction prices published. Disposition partially documented (Evera repossessed 500, Refex deal collapsed, Uber shelved absorption).
19. **Tata Xpres-T fleet traction**: Registration data provisional (369 units, Sep 2026, secondary extraction). No audited fleet/retail split.
20. **Airport charging access**: Public charging exists on premises but access rights, tariffs, bay allocation for third-party fleets are unpublished.

## Conflicting sources
- **AAI passenger figure**: Secondary compilation sums to 195.94M; deck claims 196.24M. Difference of 0.3M (0.15%). Exact figure unverified (PDF inaccessible).
- **Everest fleet**: ESG report (FY24-25) shows 2,000+ EV + 18,000+ CNG = 20,000+. Company now reports 23,500+. Gap of 3,500+ unexplained (new acquisitions or reclassification).
- **Bharat Taxi at NMIA**: Company says 600 cabs phase 1 (national). Airport announcement says "up to 40 dedicated vehicles." Different scopes (national vs. single airport).
- **Rapido cab rides**: 320,000 (Motilal Oswal, Jan 2025) vs. ~6.5M daily rides all-modes (Sensor Tower, Mar 2026). Different scopes (cab-only vs. all modes).
- **Dzire Tour S pricing**: Previously cited ₹6.79–7.74L. Official Maruti pricing ₹6.24–7.10L. Fleet pricing may differ from retail.
- **GST on ICE cars**: Deck stated 18%. Correct for small cars (≤1,200cc, ≤4,000mm). Mid-size/large cars face 28%+cess (up to 50%). Dzire Tour S qualifies as small car.

## Unsupported claims (must not appear on slide)
- "Airport has the highest margin" — not established. Airport has lowest revenue per paid trip-km (₹17.1) of five archetypes.
- "61% intra-city market share" — no trip-purpose classification exists.
- "19.14% airport CAGR" — unreconciled with 26% table figure.
- "Uber+Rapido ₹29/day" — incorrect. Rapido auto ₹9–29, cab ₹40. Uber commission-based.
- "GMR operates 200+ EV fleet at Hyderabad" — conflation. 200+ taxis announced, not EVs. OHM operates EV fleet.
- "BluSmart assumed 10%, realised 26% residual" — management statement, not audited.
- "Procurement is consistently asset-light" — mixed models documented.
- "No airport offers general depot charging" — absence of evidence, not evidence of absence.
- "Airport app-cabs are 20–30% of ground transport" — assumption, not measured.
